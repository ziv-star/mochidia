The short version

You can learn without a MochiDia account. Your guest learning stays on your device unless you choose to share a copy. Use a nickname and keep passwords, birthdays, contact details and other people’s information out of answers and reports.

Children, parent-managed learners and people whose age or eligibility is unknown cannot upload a MochiDia iCloud backup. Optional backup is for eligible personal adult learners and requires separate consent. Apple-required age checks for app access are a different step.

The free app can show a brief, family-friendly Google advert after a finished lesson; an optional Apple subscription removes adverts. There are no public profiles, chats or app-operated learning analytics. MochiDia does not record your microphone. An in-app content report is a private learning note, not a message to support.

Learning and profiles on your device

MochiDia saves answers, completed lessons, effort XP, practice results, learning dates and timezone, unfinished work, settings and private content-report notes. Local profiles contain a nickname, profile type and relevant management choices. Local privacy choices record an age declaration and policy timestamp, not your birthday or an identity document.

People with access to your unlocked device may access local profiles. The app does not automatically copy guest learning into an iCloud profile. Connecting to Apple uses a scoped identifier and an account-specific local namespace to keep records separate; MochiDia does not receive your Apple password or email address.

Learning saves keep three rotating local recovery copies. A reviewed restore may keep a device archive, and damaged save bytes can be retained separately for recovery. These copies have no inactivity expiry. Device-only records remain until removed through available profile controls, removal of app storage or uninstall. Guest and iCloud profile deletion have different scopes.

The app excludes its learning storage and app-owned diagnostic fingerprint directory from iOS system backup. This does not erase historical phone backups or files you exported. Losing your phone or removing the app can lose device-only learning. Support cannot recover it remotely.

Apple age checks

Apple presents its own age-sharing interface. When applicable Apple regulatory features require a check for app access, MochiDia uses that separate check. An unavailable required check shows retry/help and preserves saved learning. On older unsupported systems, protected local learning is available; optional adult cloud backup remains unavailable without verified eligibility.

The app receives coarse age/eligibility and parental-control facts in session memory, not your birthday or verification documents. Facts are invalidated when the app backgrounds, profiles change or the app restarts; backup eligibility expires after five minutes and is rechecked before writes. Raw Apple age facts are not saved into learning, cloud backups, reports or exports.

An adult declaration or guardian checkbox is not proof of identity or verified parental consent. An Apple Account’s age does not establish the age of every learner using that device. Age sharing alone does not authorize backup, advertising, purchases or a future significant app update.

Optional private iCloud backup

Choosing to connect can contact Apple for account status, the private learner catalog and requested downloads, before you choose to upload a backup. A remembered connection may reconnect when the app opens. Connecting does not automatically upload your guest learning.

An eligible personal adult learner can separately opt into backup. It can include profile identifiers, nickname/type, backup choices and learning records, including answers, drafts, dates and private content-report notes. It uses your private MochiDia CloudKit container and iCloud storage allowance, with no public record grants. Learning payloads use CloudKit encrypted fields. We do not promise end-to-end encryption, a fixed hosting country or control over all Apple service retention.

Apple manages Apple Account authentication, recovery and its service infrastructure. Its privacy policy and iCloud terms apply to its services. Apple also processes operational account and service information under its own terms. We use Apple’s infrastructure for the optional app backup rather than operating a MochiDia password service.

Stopping backup prevents new app uploads. A request already sent can finish, and prior backups remain until you explicitly delete them. Cloud learning has no automatic inactivity expiry. Selected synthetic iPhone/iPad tests have verified automatic saving and recovery, including offline retry and conflicts. This is not a guarantee against every loss. Keep important exports private.

Exports, recovery and deletion

Export controls let you preview the selected learner’s readable backup. Continuing opens a system destination that you choose after a separate destination review. The app removes its temporary export file after the share operation returns. File import uses a file you select; system-created import copies and recipient caches may remain under their own policies. Integrity hashes do not encrypt an export.

A person or app receiving a shared file may retain it; MochiDia cannot recall that copy. Children should ask a parent or trusted adult for help before sharing. Our review screen does not verify parental consent.

Confirmed iCloud-profile deletion removes the reachable MochiDia profile and learning copies within that deletion scope. A minimal random deletion marker remains to reject stale uploads. Offline devices can retain old copies until they reconnect. Shared exports, historical system backups and Apple’s operational retention are separate. Deletion does not remove your Apple Account, other apps’ records or your email account.

Local recovery copies and reviewed restore archives for a selected workspace are included in its applicable deletion controls. A guest workspace does not have the same account-deletion control; removing account records is not a blanket deletion of guest learning.

Content reports, support and privacy requests

An in-app report stores lesson/exercise references, a category, optional detail and status/date in the selected learner’s records. It is not automatically emailed to us. For an opted-in adult iCloud learner, those private notes can accompany the learning backup. Keep personal information out of the report.

If you email us, you share your sender address, message and attachments with the publisher named above, our AgentMail provider and your chosen mail provider. Send a short issue description and, if useful, the app version, device model and iOS version. Do not send passwords, birthdays, identity documents, payment details, recordings, full learning backups or someone else’s records. Children should ask a parent or trusted adult to contact us.

We keep support correspondence while handling the request and aim to remove unnecessary ticket content within 90 days of closure. Minimal rights/incident records may be kept for up to 12 months where needed to demonstrate how a request was handled, or longer if required by law or an active dispute. These are operator retention targets, not a promise that all provider recovery copies vanish at those times.

AgentMail’s privacy policy describes US processing, messages until deletion, recovery copies for up to 35 days, metrics for up to 90 days and most logs for up to 365 days. Some raw message/attachment objects have no automatic expiry. Its subprocessor list describes further providers. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information in mail; contact us for applicable transfer and retention details.

Why information is used

Where UK/EU data-protection law applies, we rely on consent for optional adult backup, which you can withdraw for future uploads through the backup controls. We rely on legitimate interests in providing requested local learning, protecting saved work, separating profiles and giving proportionate assistance, taking learners’ privacy and children’s interests into account. Required legal duties, including applicable privacy requests and mandatory age/access requirements, are addressed under the relevant legal obligation. We do not treat a local age declaration as parental consent.

Providing a nickname and learning answers is part of using the relevant local features; you can use guest learning instead of optional backup. Do not provide sensitive information. No automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made by lesson scoring. Required age/eligibility checks can restrict access or optional backup as described above.

Your choices and rights

You can keep learning local, stop future backup uploads, preview/export selected records and use the relevant deletion controls. Where applicable, you can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact the privacy email or postal address above. We may need proportionate verification, but will not ask for your Apple password. We normally cannot identify or retrieve a guest record kept only on your phone; we can explain the available device controls.

We handle requests under the deadlines and exceptions applicable where you live. You may complain to your local data-protection authority; UK concerns can be raised with the Information Commissioner’s Office. Children retain their applicable rights. Asking an adult for help does not itself authorize disclosure of another learner’s records.

Audio, diagnostics and security

Japanese playback uses Apple’s device speech service through Expo Speech. We do not record audio or send text to an app-operated speech server. System voice downloads, Apple diagnostics and service communications are governed by Apple. Media volume and the selected output route control playback; the ringer’s Silent Mode does not suppress lesson audio.

App-owned preservation diagnostics contain record hashes and sizes, not raw answers, and have no uploader. They remain locally until relevant storage removal and have no automatic expiry. Local integrity/recovery tools reduce some save risks; they do not guarantee that every loss or security incident can be recovered. Apple’s private storage and scoped app controls are not a guarantee of absolute security.

Google advertising

All lessons stay free. The free app can show a brief Google AdMob banner after a saved lesson, when you choose to leave the completed lesson. The limit is one opportunity per 30 accumulated active-learning minutes, lasting at most 15 seconds including loading. You can continue without waiting. Idle/background time does not accumulate opportunities. No advert is requested during a question, audio, save or recovery.

MochiDia does not ask for your age. Adverts are limited to family-friendly (general audience) content and are requested as non-personalised adverts for everyone. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before an advert is requested, and Profile includes a link to change advertising privacy choices later. Learning remains available whatever you choose, and when no advert is available. The app keeps an advertising pacing counter on this installation, separate from learner backups.

Google's Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform SDKs handle eligible advertising and privacy-choice requests. Google may receive an IP address and approximate location, device identifiers, advertising views/interactions, crash and performance information, and other interactions used for advertising or analytics. MochiDia does not attach learner nicknames, answers, Japanese exercises, XP, progress, CloudKit identifiers or exports to these requests. We do not request precise location for advertising. Non-personalized requests and disabling publisher first-party ID do not mean no information is processed. Google handles its service information under its privacy policy and advertising information. Google describes SDK collection in its app data-disclosure guidance.

The selected advertising partner is Google Advertising Products; automatic partner additions are off. Before an eligible request, Google's privacy interface presents applicable choices. Profile's “Change advertising privacy choices” link lets you review or withdraw your choices at any time. Withdrawal stops new advertising requests that depend on consent; a request already sent may finish. SDK privacy choices may be kept locally by Google, and withdrawal does not erase information already processed by Google. We do not control or promise a fixed retention period, hosting country or deletion outcome for Google's service records.

Optional ad-free subscription

The monthly and yearly Apple subscriptions remove adverts only; all lessons remain free. The UK prices configured with Apple are £1.00 per month and £7.00 per year; other storefronts use Apple's equivalent local prices. The purchase screen shows Apple's actual localized price and billing period before a purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple; cancellation normally stops future renewal rather than ending the already paid period. Apple handles billing, payment information, refunds, subscription management and App Store account recovery. MochiDia does not operate a payment server or receive your card number.

The app uses verified StoreKit transaction and subscription status on this device to determine ad-free access. Paid status belongs to the Apple App Store purchasing account, not a learner nickname or iCloud progress file. A learning export cannot grant paid access. Restore purchases and Manage subscription are explicit actions; they contact Apple's services. Apple's purchase sheet confirms every purchase, and Apple's Family Sharing settings (such as Ask to Buy) apply to children's accounts. The app does not include payment receipts or paid flags in learning backups. Apple's Standard EULA and Apple's privacy policy apply to their respective services.
