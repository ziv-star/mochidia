Help with MochiDia

For learning, accessibility or privacy questions, email japanese-learning-lab-support@agentmail.to. MochiDia is being prepared for its first App Store release.

Tell us what you tried and what happened. The app version, device model and iOS version can help. Do not send passwords, birthdays, identity documents, payment details, recordings, a full learning backup or another person’s information. Children should ask a parent or trusted adult for help. The contact link opens your mail app; it does not send anything automatically.

A lesson or reading looks wrong

Use the content-report control to record the lesson/exercise and issue privately in your learner’s records. Reports in the app are not automatically sent to a support team. To tell us about an issue, email a brief description and the lesson title or identifier; no learning backup is needed.

Reading help is on by default. You can turn furigana off using the reading-help switch. Learning materials are a study aid and do not guarantee JLPT proficiency or an exam result.

I cannot hear Japanese audio

Tap Play or Replay and check your phone’s media volume and selected output route. Disconnect unintended headphones or speakers if needed. Silent Mode for the ringer does not suppress lesson playback; media volume still controls it, and MochiDia does not raise the volume automatically. A text transcript remains available. If playback continues unexpectedly, use Stop playback.

MochiDia does not record your microphone or grade pronunciation from a recording.

Saving and recovering learning

Guest progress, drafts and preferences stay on this installation. Three rotating recovery copies can help with damaged saves; they do not protect against losing the phone or removing the app. MochiDia excludes learning storage from system backup. Before changing devices or removing the app, preview a separate export and keep any copy private.

Optional private iCloud backup is for an eligible personal adult learner after a local declaration, a current Apple check and a separate backup choice. Connecting does not automatically upload guest learning. Child, managed, unknown, declined or unsupported eligibility states cannot upload. Selected synthetic iPhone/iPad checks have verified automatic saving and recovery, including offline retry and conflicts. They do not guarantee recovery from every device loss. Keep an important export private.

Stopping uploads keeps prior cloud copies until explicit deletion. A reviewed restore can preserve a device archive. Account deletion affects the relevant reachable MochiDia records, not your Apple Account. Offline devices, shared files, past backups and provider recovery copies may retain earlier records. Review the selected learner and privacy notice before a destructive action. Support cannot remotely recover a lost guest save.

Apple account or age-check help

Apple manages Apple Account sign-in, age sharing and recovery. MochiDia will never ask for your Apple password, birthday or identity document. External Apple help offers Cancel before opening Apple’s website. Age sharing does not turn on backup.

If an Apple-required app-access check cannot finish, retry without clearing saved learning. Ask an adult or support for help without sending identity documents. Do not change your real Apple age or country to bypass a restriction.

Privacy requests and correspondence

MochiDia’s publisher, named in the privacy notice, is the privacy contact. You can ask about access, correction, export, deletion, withdrawal or other rights applicable where you live using the email above.

We handle requests under applicable deadlines. No guaranteed support response time is advertised. Please read the privacy notice for information about support-mail processing and retention.

Adverts and the ad-free subscription

All lessons stay free. The free app can show a brief, family-friendly Google advert after you finish a lesson and choose Continue: at most once per 30 active-learning minutes and for at most 15 seconds including loading. Continue without waiting is always available. MochiDia does not ask for your age; adverts are non-personalised and limited to general-audience content. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices first, and Profile has a “Change advertising privacy choices” link. If an advert appears while you are answering, playing audio or saving, please report the screen and app version without sending a backup.

The optional ad-free subscription (UK £1.00 per month or £7.00 per year; Apple shows the local price) removes adverts only. Find it under Profile, “Remove adverts”. Restore purchases checks the Apple App Store purchasing account, which may differ from the iCloud account. Manage subscription opens Apple's controls; removing MochiDia does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles billing and refund requests, and Ask to Buy applies to children's Apple accounts. Never email card information, receipts, passwords or a verification code to MochiDia support.

Read the privacy notice for the Google SDK data types and withdrawal limits.
